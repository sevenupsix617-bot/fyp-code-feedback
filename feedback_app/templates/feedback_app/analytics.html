Question Evidence

Question Difficulty Overview

This table uses student question attempts only; the student-level summaries above exclude staff experiments. Average attempts to solve counts submissions up to the first solved attempt in each student practice run, then averages solved runs only. More attempts to solve can signal difficulty; total attempts alone can reflect popularity. Unsolved runs stay in the solve-rate denominator.

Popular, easier: at least 5 attempts by 2 students, at least 75% of runs solved, and an average of at most 2 attempts to solve. Difficult: at least 3 attempts to solve on average, or at least 5 attempts with a solve rate of 50% or lower. Other attempted questions have limited evidence. A mismatch ran successfully but printed the wrong output; runtime errors are exceptions, while other failures include syntax errors and timeouts.
